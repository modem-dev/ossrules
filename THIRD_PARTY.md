documentation from Modem Labs Inc. It does not relicense material copied from
other projects.

## Instructions, skills, and excerpts

- `public/files/` contains pinned upstream instruction files and supporting
  documents. Project manifests record source revisions and known license details.
- `content/skill-files/` contains upstream skill resources stored by Git blob
  hash. `content/skills/` records their origins, snapshots, and discovery metadata.
- Editorial entries in `content/projects/` include verbatim upstream quotations.
  Those quotations and upstream descriptions retain their original terms.

Consult the original repository and any file-specific notices at the recorded
revision. Missing license metadata is not a grant of rights under this project's
MIT license. Preserve bundled notices and source attribution when redistributing
third-party material.
